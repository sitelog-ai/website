About the founder

Built by someone who's stood where you're standing.

SiteLog AI comes out of thirty years on real jobsites — not a software company's guess at what the field needs.

Thirty years building commercial, multifamily, and luxury construction taught me one thing: a job lives or dies on its documentation — and too often that record sits in one super's head and one super's phone, and walks out the door the day he leaves. I built SiteLog AI to do one thing better than anything in the industry — put the day on the record in seconds, accurately — so it belongs to the company and stays there. It's the tool the field has needed for years.

Louis Armanini
Founder, SiteLog AI · 30 years building commercial, multifamily & luxury construction

Why the field needed this

Every super knows the drill: the day ends, the paperwork doesn't. Daily logs get written at 9 pm from memory, or skipped. Photos pile up in a camera roll nobody can search. And when a back-charge or a delay claim lands months later, the company goes looking for a record that was never really kept.

SiteLog AI is the answer a builder gives to that problem, not the answer a software company gives: capture the day in thirty seconds, standing on the job — snap the photos, say what happened, tap a type — and the record files itself, builds the daily report, and answers questions in plain English for the life of the project.

The company

SiteLog AI is a product of Block Management Services LLC, a Florida construction project management and software company. The guides and articles on this site are written from field experience — the same practices used on real commercial, multifamily, and luxury projects.
