What belongs in a daily log

A log that's missing the right lines is just a diary. These are the entries that settle arguments later — the template has a section for each.

MANPOWER — EVERY DAY

Every trade, the count, and the hours — including the crew that didn't show. Headcounts are what delay claims are built from.

WHERE · WHO · WHAT

Say the area, name who did it, describe the condition. A note you can't place on the job is a note you can't use.

DELAYS, THE DAY THEY HAPPEN

What stopped, who it stopped, and the day you were back. A week summarized after the fact gets picked apart.

EXTRA WORK / T&M

Who directed it, and the crew, equipment and hours it took. Without a name, the record reads like your crew decided to spend the money.

SAFETY — EVEN "NONE"

Items called out and incidents. Writing "none" daily proves you were watching; a blank proves nothing.

SIGN IT, DATE IT

A signed page made that day carries weight a reconstruction never will. That's the whole point of a daily log.
