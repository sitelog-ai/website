Accessibility at SiteLog AI

Last updated: August 1, 2026

SiteLog AI is built for people who work with their hands, outdoors, in gloves, in glare, and in a hurry — accessibility isn't a legal checkbox for us, it's the product. We are committed to making sitelog-ai.com and the SiteLog AI service usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. Concretely, on this site that means: text alternatives on images, a keyboard-operable interface with visible focus indicators, labeled form fields with status messages announced to assistive technology, semantic page structure with landmarks and a skip-to-content link, and text colors checked to meet WCAG contrast minimums.

Where we are

This site is a small set of static pages, reviewed against WCAG 2.1 AA in August 2026. Accessibility is an ongoing effort — content changes, and standards evolve — so we re-check when we make significant changes.

Found a barrier? Tell us — we'll fix it.

Email support@sitelog-ai.com with the subject "Accessibility feedback."

Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology you were using. A person reads every message, and we aim to respond within 5 business days.

Third-party services

Some parts of the service open pages we don't control — for example, Stripe's secure billing pages, the Apple App Store, and Google Play. Those providers publish their own accessibility commitments. If you hit a barrier there while using SiteLog, tell us anyway — we'll help you complete what you were trying to do and pass the report along.
