Owner questions

The things an owner asks before signing off.

Who can see whose logs?
On a team account the logs belong to the company. As admin you assign each job to a super, and each job keeps its own logs, its own daily records, and its own Vault — so one job's history never bleeds into another's. You control who's on the account and which jobs they run.
What happens to our record if we cancel?
Your record stays readable and exportable — every report is emailed to you as a PDF for exactly this reason, so the history lives in your own inbox, not locked in ours. We will never hold your record hostage. Cancel anytime from the dashboard's Manage billing; service runs to the end of the period you've paid for.
How do seats and billing work?
Seats count themselves. Every teammate you add is a billed seat at $24.99/mo — and your own seat is free unless you put a job on it. Add a super mid-month and the bill prorates automatically; remove one and it prorates down. No seat minimum, no seat math.
Can a super quietly edit or delete a log?
No — by design. Once a log is filed it can't be altered, and each day is sealed overnight into a tamper-evident daily record. That's what makes the record carry weight with owners, architects, insurers, and lawyers. A correction is filed as a new log, exactly how paper daily logs handled it for a hundred years.
Who owns the data, and is it secure?
On a team account, logs captured under company jobsites belong to the company. Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to your authenticated account, and we never sell your data or use your content to train AI models. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy and Terms.
What does my crew have to do to get started?
Almost nothing. The moment you invite someone, we email them the links to download SiteLog AI from the App Store or Google Play and tell them to sign in with their work email — their assigned jobs are already waiting, and their seat is covered, so nobody on your crew ever enters a card. That's the whole setup.
