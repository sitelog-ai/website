The Record

Two ways the day goes on the record.

A sealed daily record no one can rewrite — automatic, every night. SiteLog AI closes out every workday on its own: the day's logs — every note, photo, and timestamp — are saved as a dated daily report. You don't build it and you can't forget it — each day is sealed overnight into a tamper-evident record, and filed logs can't be edited by design.

On demand, any time. Pull any slice yourself — today, this week, the whole job — all categories or just the open RFIs — and email it as a PDF to the owner, the office, or your own file. The nightly record has your back; the on-demand report gets it where it needs to go.

Day after day, those reports become the project's archive — complete and searchable. When a delay claim, a back-charge, or an insurance question shows up months later, you don't reconstruct it from memory. You open that day and hand it over.
